Lifo — a revision app built around active recall

Lifo turns lecture notes or a topic prompt into bite-sized courses and quizzes, then helps university students return to the material through spaced review, progress insights, and study goals.

Explore Lifo
01

Make a course from class material

Generate structured lessons from typed topics or uploaded lecture files, with source references for notes-based questions.

02

Practise, then revisit what needs work

Short quizzes, spaced repetition, due-question reviews, weak-topic sessions, and recall insights turn study into a repeatable loop.

03

Keep motivation social and optional

Daily goals, quests, streaks, friends, and course leagues add momentum; course sharing is opt-in and keeps personal progress private.

04

Build with privacy and cost controls

Firebase services handle accounts and study data, while server-side AI quotas, consent, privacy settings, and data export/deletion shape the product.
